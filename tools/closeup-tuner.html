Close-up tuner

Every consonant's close-up, side by side with the whole head, so you can see what each crop throws away. Drag the sliders until it reads well to a five-year-old, then Copy the table at the bottom into anatomy.js. Two sounds made in the same place can want different framing — /t/ and /s/ are both alveolar but a tap and a held hiss don't sit well at the same zoom — so each phoneme gets its own entry.
