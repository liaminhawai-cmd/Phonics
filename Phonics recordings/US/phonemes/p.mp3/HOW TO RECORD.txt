PHONEME: /p/ as in pen

This is a stop consonant — say the shortest possible release,
no vowel tacked on (say /p/, not "puh").

Example words that use this sound: pen, map, happy

General rules (same for every folder):
- One take per file. Say it once, naturally.
- Quiet room, phone voice-memo is genuinely fine.
- Any filename and format is OK in here (m4a, wav, mp3...) — just one file per folder.
- Trim dead silence off the start/end if you can; not essential.
