What it can and can't hear

It can hear: whether the voice is switched on (/s/ vs /z/, /f/ vs /v/), whether air is rushing at all, and whether a hiss is the thin high kind (/s/) or the fat low kind (/sh/).
It cannot hear: /f/ from /th/, or /p/ from /t/ from /k/. Those differences are almost invisible to a microphone — they are carried by the eye. So the app shows the picture and asks the child, rather than inventing a verdict it can't support.
A “?” in the read-out means the measurement landed in no-man's-land. That is a real answer, not a failure.
