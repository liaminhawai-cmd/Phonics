# Words to record — checklist

**196 unique words.** Record each word once, say it clearly (Australian).
Save each as `activities/audio/words/<word>.mp3` — **the filename is just the word**
(all lowercase, e.g. `nan.mp3`, `stop.mp3`, `fish.mp3`). mp3/m4a/wav/mp4 all fine.

A word that appears in more than one level only needs recording **once** — it's reused.

The app plays these per word, so you can build any worksheet from them.
