One grid, two walls

Sounds × spellings form a grid. The sound wall sums each ROW (spelling depth); the bookmark sums each COLUMN (reading depth + hand). Rings are SEGMENTED — one slice per taught pairing — so teaching a new spelling adds an empty slice instead of erasing progress.

Sound wall ring = row
/ay/
Three taught spellings = three slices. ay and ai are owned (spelled from hearing); ea is the empty slice — tap the ring and that's what it offers to practise. Closes when the row is all gold. Level 9 teaching a new spelling adds a fourth slice — progress is never erased, the ring just grows a gap.
Bookmark ring = column + hand
ea
One blue slice per sound this spelling makes (/ee/ read ✓, /ay/ and /e/ still open) plus one green slice for handwriting (worst of e + a letter scores). Closes when the column is read every way AND the hand can write it. The bookmark cell goes gold with the ring.

Spelling lives on the sound wall (encode) · reading + hand live on the bookmark (decode + form) · the per-tile three-ring view remains as the tap-through detail.
